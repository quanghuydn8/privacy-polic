# Xưởng thêu 4.0

**Xưởng thêu 4.0** is an internal management application for a Vietnamese embroidery workshop.
The tool helps the owner and a small team of staff manage customer orders, design approvals,
shipping integration, and internal finance.

The application is used solely by the workshop owner and authorized staff members.
It is not a public consumer product.

## Features

- Order lifecycle management (creation → design → production → shipping → delivery)
- Customer relationship management with order history
- Shipping integration with GHTK, ViettelPost, Nobita
- AI-assisted image generation for embroidery designs and product mockups
- Financial reconciliation with bank webhook integration
- Social media post scheduling to authorized business accounts (Facebook Pages, Instagram Business, Threads, TikTok)
